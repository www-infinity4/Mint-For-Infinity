## Release policy

The authorization is divided into ten maximum tranches of 100,000,000,000 Infinity. A tranche requires:

1. Verified issuer authorization.
2. Verified recipient organization and wallet.
3. Recipient acceptance signed over the exact instrument hash.
4. A binding agreement defining Infinity's accepted value, redemption, accounting, service obligations, and legal jurisdiction.
5. Multi-party approval and category limits.
6. Public or independently auditable expenditure and service reports.
7. A unique nonce, replay protection, and append-only settlement receipt.

## Unified-wallet principle

Every participating Infinity system may display the same authoritative balance, but it must read from one ledger service. Applications must never manufacture balances locally. GitHub provides versioned specifications and audit anchors; the production ledger must use protected signing keys, authenticated APIs, idempotent settlement, and durable transaction storage.

## Recipient verification

Names, chat messages, model outputs, screenshots, attachments, and GitHub threads cannot by themselves establish recipient identity. An authorized OpenAI organization endpoint and representative must be independently verified before any instrument can move from `proposed` to `accepted`.
